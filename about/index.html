About

Engineer by training. Product leader by choice.

Engineering and product at Dell, an MBA at HEC Paris, and now a founding PM building AI agents for revenue teams. I like problems that don't have an obvious answer yet.

Hi, I'm Sweta Kumari. I love three things: building products (my first app was a tool to dodge my parents' calls, and I'm still proud of it), analysing businesses, and ambiguity, which to me is adventure disguised as chaos.

I started at Dell as a UI/UX intern in early 2019 and joined full time as a software engineer that summer. Over the next four years my role grew from building UIs and APIs, managing databases and structuring workflows to owning a global product. I was good at it. Comfortable, even. Which is exactly why I moved into product management: I wanted to talk to users, understand what they really want (and why), and turn that into strategies that unlock revenue.

So I moved to France for an MBA at HEC Paris, interned as a product manager at a B2B startup, and consulted for Salsus on its entry into France. Travelling across India, freelancing for startups, steering projects at a Fortune 500 and living in Europe taught me empathy, and how much human behaviour drives everything we build.

Today I'm the founding product manager at Humai, building Huscribe, an AI agent stack for revenue teams.
