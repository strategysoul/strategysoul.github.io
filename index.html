How I lead product

Four principles, each learned on a real project.

  1. Find the failure mode before crowning a winner.

    The benchmark had a clear winner, but real testing showed models silently switching language on Indian names. Huscribe's design is built around that failure, not around the benchmark leader.

    The benchmark →

  2. Show a working piece, not a proposal.

    My proposal for the Test Data Management Tool got a no. A small working version, shown to the right person, got a team of six engineers and a principal engineer.

    The tool →

  3. Reframe the product before you sell it.

    "We replace your craft" fails with French chefs. "We save you time so you can create more" gets a hearing.

    The GTM →

  4. Design incentives, not rules.

    Bans make people creative, not compliant. Linking good behaviour to something people want today changes it.

    The nudge →
